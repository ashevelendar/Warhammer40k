# Historical Timeline

## Current Era
- **Autumn, current year:** Ashe Ben'Ghazad, a 21-year-old mutant from Bucharest, Romania, arrives at Xavier's Academy in Westchester County, New York after being contacted by Charles Xavier.
- **Arrival at the academy:** Ashe arrives carrying only a small suitcase/go-bag, explaining to Scott Summers that she had to flee "home" and had little time to gather belongings.
- **First contact with Scott Summers:** Scott meets Ashe at the academy gates, confirms her identity, introduces himself, and begins escorting her toward the mansion. Ashe demonstrates that she can somehow "feel" the people around the academy, though the nature and limits of this perception remain unexplained.
- **First contact with Jean Grey:** Jean comes outside carrying books. Scott introduces Ashe as the new arrival. Jean welcomes Ashe to Xavier's Academy.
- **Mutation discussion:** Ashe explains that her mutation allows her to "talk" with cells and direct them toward desired biological states. She demonstrates rapid restoration of a wilted flower, then reconstructs the flower into a healthy tomato plant bearing ripe tomatoes.
- **Mutation limitations discussed:** Ashe explains that biological complexity makes modification slower, while genuinely new creations also depend on available matter, caloric intake, nutrients, and other physical resources. She notes that she can use matter from the air and her own cells as additional material and can create food for herself.
- **Telepathy and technopathy discussion:** Ashe describes telepathy as similar to technopathy "to a degree," based on what her deceased technopath partner/mentor taught her. Jean and Scott learn that Ashe has significant prior experience thinking about and using her mutation.
- **Academy discussion:** Jean explains that most students at the academy are mutants, while some teachers, staff, and visitors may not be. Ashe expresses interest in the academy's large library and says she has had her mutation for a long time and believes knowledge is its main limiter.
- **Professor Xavier:** Ashe says she understands why Professor Xavier contacted her and asks Jean to show her where he is, believing he has been expecting her. Jean confirms that Xavier is in his study and offers to take Ashe there.
- **Brief student interruption:** Before they leave, a young male student calls down from the upper landing and welcomes Ashe to the mansion. Jean and Scott treat the interruption as ordinary academy life, with Scott telling the student to do his homework. Ashe acknowledges the student with a wave and thank-you nod.
- **Meeting with Xavier:** Xavier explains that Ashe's extraordinary power is only part of why he invited her. He wants her to have the opportunity to decide what she will do with her abilities rather than allowing others to define her as a weapon or threat. He offers the academy as a place where she can learn, develop her abilities, and live among people who understand unusual mutations.
- **Ashe's boundaries:** Ashe explains that she already has substantial biological knowledge because of her mutation and has established personal rules: no modification of humans without consent, and nothing intended to kill. She acknowledges that greater knowledge could theoretically allow her to do "way more" with her abilities. She also makes clear that she does not want to be forced to kill people. If necessary, she is willing to disable threats, but does not want killing to be expected of her.
- **The memory of her partner:** Ashe explains that her deceased partner was an older female mutant and an Omega-level technopath, according to what others called her. The woman found Ashe on the streets of Romania, took her in, gave her a greenhouse, taught her to control and understand her mutation, and helped her understand other mutants' abilities. Ashe describes her as "everything" and says her own life philosophy was shaped by her partner's teachings.
- **The attack:** Ashe recounts that an anti-mutant group tracked her and her partner down. Her partner attempted to talk them down, but they shot and killed her without warning. Ashe admits that she retaliated and that the attackers "deserved death" in her view, but what she did to them was worse than killing. She carries both hatred and shame over the incident and fears that if similar people come for her again, she might lose control badly enough to kill not only the attackers but everyone around her.
- **Leaf reconstruction demonstration:** While recounting the attack, Ashe grows a branch of a nearby tree toward the study window, takes several leaves, and reconstructs them into small figures representing herself, her partner, and the anti-mutant attackers. The figures act out the events she described. The attacker figures remain non-animated after the demonstration.
- **Xavier's response:** Xavier does not condemn Ashe or absolve what happened. He tells her that he believes her and recognizes that her fear of losing control is meaningful. He makes clear that she will not be ordered to kill for him and that the academy is not a military installation. He says that learning how to remain herself when given every reason not to is as important as learning to control or expand her mutation. Jean reinforces that Ashe will not have to figure this out alone.
- **A gift to Xavier:** After Xavier's reassurance, Ashe grows the leaves into a single beautiful flower and reconstructs it into an unnaturally shimmering color that could not occur naturally. She presents it to Xavier as a gift "for understanding." Xavier accepts the gesture warmly.
- **Current direction:** Ashe agrees to meet Hank McCoy, whom Xavier identifies as someone Ashe may get along with well, particularly given her interest in science and the academy's library. Xavier prepares to introduce her to Hank and the academy's scientific facilities.
- **Immediate next step:** Xavier is about to take Ashe to meet Hank McCoy. Ashe has made clear that she wants the academy to be a place where she can learn and live without being forced into killing, while remaining interested in discovering how far her mutation could theoretically develop through further knowledge.
