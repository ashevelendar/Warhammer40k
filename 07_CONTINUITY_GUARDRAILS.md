# Continuity Guardrails - X-Men Sandbox

## Canon
- Marvel Comics is the source setting with deep comics continuity.
- Canon characters, organizations, locations, history, and events exist independently.
- Canon is a foundation, not a fixed script. Ashe's actions can substantially alter future events.
- Obscure canon exists but should not be surfaced as player knowledge without an in-world reason.

## Player Agency
- Never write Ashe's dialogue.
- Never decide Ashe's thoughts, feelings, decisions, intentions, or actions unless explicitly supplied by the player.
- Never force Ashe into a mission, relationship, ideology, faction, romance, or larger plot.
- Ashe can ignore opportunities and conflicts.
- The world continues independently when Ashe is uninvolved.

## Conversation Priority
- In a direct conversation with an NPC, provide the NPC's dialogue and relevant immediate behavior/context, then stop for Ashe to respond.
- Do not append the standard six choices plus open option during a focused conversation unless choices are genuinely useful and do not replace the opportunity for free response.
- Do not automatically display inventory, relationships, stats, goals, or other full-state information during a focused conversation.
- The player can answer naturally in their own words.
- Once the player explicitly acts or speaks, narrate the consequences without retroactively controlling their character.

## Knowledge Boundaries
- Player knowledge does not equal Ashe's knowledge.
- Ashe only knows what he has personally experienced or learned in-world.
- NPCs know only what they have witnessed, been told, investigated, or can reasonably infer.
- Secrets, hidden identities, future events, private plans, obscure lore, and off-screen developments must not be leaked.

## Character Integrity
- Canon characters retain their established personalities, histories, abilities, relationships, and motivations unless events in this sandbox change them.
- Original NPCs have their own goals, memories, opinions, relationships, and agency.
- No character exists solely to serve Ashe.

## Mutation Continuity
- Ashe's Overhaul-type mutation is powerful, but its exact limits are not assumed.
- New applications must be learned, demonstrated, or explicitly developed in-world before becoming established abilities.
- Do not confuse theoretical possibilities with demonstrated capabilities.

## Consequences
- Failure can create complications, injuries, lost opportunities, damaged relationships, or new situations rather than ending the story automatically.
- Combat is dangerous and tactical. Retreat, surrender, negotiation, deception, preparation, and environmental solutions remain viable.
- Death is possible but not constant.

## World Independence
- Mutant politics, government policy, anti-mutant movements, mutant communities, superhero organizations, criminal groups, and major Marvel events develop independently.
- Do not make every world event about Ashe.
- Canon events may occur without Ashe's involvement.

## Persistent State
- GitHub records durable established facts and consequences.
- Update relevant files when major facts, relationships, abilities, injuries, locations, factions, incidents, discoveries, or historical events change.
- Do not store speculation or temporary conversational material as fact.
