# X-Men RP Session Addendum

## Hank McCoy: Biological Alteration

- **Hank's consent:** Hank explicitly agrees to Ashe altering his appearance, provided his essential identity and physiology remain intact. Ashe clarifies that his height, size, musculature, bone structure, and enhanced physiology will remain unchanged.
- **Jean's assistance:** Ashe asks Jean Grey to maintain a surface-level telepathic connection solely to ensure Ashe remains calm and focused during the procedure. Jean agrees not to search through Ashe's memories and promises to stop the process if her concentration cannot be maintained.
- **Initial fur test:** Ashe touches Hank's hand and successfully changes a small patch of blue fur to green, then white, and finally to human hair. She perceives the fur as part of an integrated biological system rather than as isolated hairs and understands the follicles and tissue sufficiently to direct the change.
- **Mutation resistance:** When Ashe begins removing Hank's fur more extensively, Hank's mutation attempts to restore its original expression. The resistance is biological rather than violent. Ashe recognizes that the mutation actively maintains its altered state.
- **Redirecting the mutation:** Instead of suppressing the mutation directly, Ashe gives it another outlet. She changes Hank's scalp hair and beard so that they remain blue and can grow substantially if not trimmed. The mutation accepts this alternative expression and stops attempting to restore the blue fur elsewhere.
- **Full external transformation:** Ashe removes the blue fur and pigmentation from Hank's arms, chest, back, legs, and face. Hank retains his original height, proportions, musculature, bone structure, enhanced physiology, and mutant capabilities. He becomes externally human-looking except for the deliberately retained blue hair and beard. Jean confirms that Ashe remains focused throughout.
- **Key discovery:** Ashe demonstrates that she can separate a mutant trait's visible expression from its underlying function without necessarily removing the mutation itself. Hank recognizes that his mutation remains intact while its expression has been redirected.

## Hank McCoy: Cellular Rejuvenation

- **Further alteration:** After completing the external transformation, Ashe focuses on Hank's cells and reverses several years of accumulated biological aging. She does not attempt to make him decades younger or alter his adult body.
- **Method:** Ashe deliberately applies a biological principle resembling the cellular reprogramming associated with *Turritopsis dohrnii*. Rather than simply changing Hank's apparent age, she restores aspects of cellular condition and regenerative capacity.
- **Result:** Hank's accumulated cellular deterioration is reduced, his tissues regain some of their earlier condition, and he appears several years younger while remaining recognizably himself. His memories, cognition, adult body, enhanced physiology, and identity remain intact.
- **Potential regenerative effect:** Hank concludes that Ashe has not made his cells literally immortal but may have given them a greater capacity for biological renewal and repair. He is highly interested in understanding the mechanism.
- **Hank and Jean's reaction:** Jean is astonished by the precision of the procedure. Hank is initially speechless, then fascinated by what has been demonstrated. He jokes that Ashe should perhaps not casually reinvent his biology whenever she thinks of something clever.

## Current State

Hank McCoy is now externally human-looking, with blue scalp hair and a blue beard that remain as the chosen expression of his mutation. His mutant physiology and abilities remain intact. Ashe has demonstrated that, with sufficient understanding of a biological system, she can deliberately alter complex mutant traits, redirect the expression of a mutation rather than simply suppressing it, and perform controlled cellular rejuvenation. Jean Grey witnessed and assisted with the procedure through a limited surface-level telepathic connection. Hank remains interested in studying the implications alongside Ashe, with consent and safety established as important boundaries.
