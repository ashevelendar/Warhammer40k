# Nation Roleplay Sandbox

A persistent, living-world nation roleplay.

The world exists independently of the player's realm. Nations expand, decline, trade, feud, migrate, discover, collapse and reform whether the player becomes involved or not.

## Core principles

- Player agency comes first.
- The player's realm is not inherently special.
- No forced quests, prophecies, wars or obligations.
- Multiple opportunities can exist simultaneously.
- The player may ignore any opportunity.
- Consequences persist.
- NPCs, factions and nations have their own goals.
- Failure creates new circumstances rather than simply ending the campaign.
- Quiet periods, governance, trade, construction and ordinary life matter as much as conflict.

## Realm creation

The starting setup records:

- Realm name
- Leader
- Origin
- Culture
- Government
- Dominant and minority species
- Immediate ambition
- Six realm attributes
- Starting population, treasury, food and resources
- Initial settlements and territory

## Realm attributes

Five standard attributes are used:

- Military
- Diplomacy
- Economy
- Knowledge
- Infrastructure

The sixth is a unique attribute derived from the realm's history and culture.

Attributes range from 1 to 20 and influence outcomes consistently.

## World settings

The world records:

- Setting type
- Tone
- Danger level
- Frequency of collapse and failure
- Realism versus grand drama
- Worldbuilding answers
- Independent world developments
