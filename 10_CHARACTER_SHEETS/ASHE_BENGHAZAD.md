# Character Sheet: Ashe Ben'Ghazad

## Identity
- Name: Ashe Ben'Ghazad
- Age: 21
- Sex: Female
- Species: Mutant
- Origin: Bucharest, Romania
- Current location: Xavier's Academy, Westchester County, New York

## Core Character Rule
Ashe is the player character. Never write Ashe's dialogue, thoughts, decisions, intentions, or actions unless the player explicitly provides them.

## Known History
- Ashe grew up in Bucharest, Romania.
- She left home and later lived with an older female mutant who became her partner and mentor.
- Her partner was an exceptionally skilled technopath and taught Ashe mutation control, biological understanding, and how to understand other mutants' powers.
- Anti-mutant humans eventually found the pair. Her partner attempted to talk to them and was shot and killed.
- Ashe retaliated. The attackers did not survive. Ashe has acknowledged that she could harm people in ways far worse than simply killing them.
- Ashe carries guilt over being unable to save her partner and has a complicated relationship with the fact that humans killed her.
- Charles Xavier contacted Ashe and invited her to the academy.

## Mutation
Ashe can perceive and manipulate living biology at extraordinary depth. She can direct cells toward desired biological states and can perceive organisms as integrated biological systems rather than isolated cells.

Known capabilities include:
- Healing and restoration.
- Reshaping living tissue.
- Altering genetic and cellular characteristics.
- Deliberate self-modification.
- Creating biological structures and organisms.
- Creating food and plants.
- Separating visible mutant expression from underlying mutant function when the system permits it.
- Engineering functional biological equivalents of some abilities in theory.

## Established Limitations / Risks
- Complexity increases difficulty and time.
- Knowledge strongly affects precision and what Ashe can deliberately accomplish.
- Large or complex biological changes can consume substantial resources and exhaust Ashe.
- Ashe can use her own reserves or external biological matter/nutrients as resources.
- She can theoretically draw resources from another organism, but doing so could leave that organism severely depleted.
- Brain modification remains theoretical and untested by Ashe. She has never deliberately modified an animal or human brain.
- Ashe does not want to suppress or remove mutant abilities and has a strong personal boundary against modifying humans without consent.
- Ashe does not want killing to be an intended use of her power.

## Demonstrated Academy Applications
- Restored and redesigned plants in the conservatory.
- Created temporary biological material designed to disappear.
- Studied and reproduced traits in plants.
- Modified Hank McCoy's external appearance while preserving his mutant physiology.
- Redirected Hank's mutant expression into blue scalp hair and beard rather than suppressing the mutation.
- Rejuvenated Hank's cellular condition by several years without changing his adult identity, cognition, or mutant physiology.
- Demonstrated reversible self-modification by creating an extra digit and an octopus-like tentacle, then restoring her normal hand.
- Created a large golden superfruit with extreme nutritional density, rapid energy availability, temporary enhancement of natural healing, and purifying properties. Two small slices are intended to constitute a full meal. Excessive consumption can be lethal, although Ashe can theoretically redistribute excess nutrients.

## Relationships / Knowledge
### Charles Xavier
- Ashe knows Xavier invited her to the academy because he wants her to decide what to do with her abilities rather than be defined as a weapon or threat.
- Xavier knows substantially more about Ashe's traumatic history than Hank currently does.

### Hank McCoy
- Hank has personally witnessed Ashe's biological manipulation and her work on his body.
- Hank knows about her biological/genetic knowledge, practical limitations, consent boundaries, exhaustion risk, functional-equivalent theory, and her fear/caution around brain modification.
- Hank does **not** know the detailed history of Ashe's deceased partner unless Ashe tells him.

### Jean Grey
- Jean has witnessed and assisted with complex biological work by maintaining a limited surface-level telepathic connection to monitor Ashe's focus.
- Jean agreed not to search Ashe's memories during that assistance.
- Further knowledge must be based on what Jean has personally witnessed or been told in-world.

### Scott Summers
- Scott met Ashe at the academy gates and escorted her inside.
- Ashe briefly assessed Scott's biology through touch.

## Current State
- Residing at Xavier's Academy.
- Has her own room in the residential wing.
- Has begun making the academy greenhouse and biological resources part of her working environment.
- Relationship with the academy staff is developing through play.
