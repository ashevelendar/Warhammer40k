# Character Sheet: Jean Grey

## Role
- Senior member of Xavier's Academy.
- Telepath and trusted member of the staff.

## What Jean Knows About Ashe
- Ashe is a young mutant who arrived at Xavier's Academy after being contacted by Charles Xavier.
- Ashe is interested in understanding her mutation and improving her control through knowledge.
- Ashe considers telepathy somewhat analogous to technopathy, based on what her deceased technopath partner/mentor taught her.
- Ashe has strong boundaries around consent and does not want to modify humans without consent.
- Ashe does not want killing to be an expected use of her mutation.

## What Jean Has Personally Witnessed
- Ashe's biological manipulation and her ability to work with complex living systems.
- Ashe's experimentation with Hank McCoy, including the external transformation and cellular rejuvenation.
- Jean maintained a limited surface-level telepathic connection during Hank's procedure to monitor Ashe's focus and help keep the process safe.
- Jean did not search Ashe's memories during that assistance and agreed to stop if she could no longer safely maintain the connection.
- Jean has seen that Ashe can become severely exhausted after extensive biological work.

## Knowledge Boundaries
- Jean only knows details about Ashe's past that she has personally witnessed, been told, or otherwise learned in-world.
- Jean does not automatically know the private details Charles Xavier knows about Ashe's partner and the events surrounding the partner's death.
- Jean does not automatically know private conversations between Ashe and other characters.
- New abilities and discoveries must be learned through observation or communication.

## Relationship With Ashe
Jean has been welcoming toward Ashe and has offered support without treating her as a weapon. She has indicated that Ashe does not have to face difficult experiences alone.

## Continuity Note
Jean's telepathy does not grant automatic access to all of Ashe's memories or knowledge. Her prior assistance was explicitly surface-level and consent-based.
