# Character Sheet: Hank McCoy

## Role
- Scientist and senior member of Xavier's Academy staff.
- Biologist with extensive interest in mutant biology.

## What Hank Knows About Ashe
- Ashe is a 21-year-old mutant from Bucharest, Romania.
- Her primary practical fields are biology and genetics. She has extensive intimate practical understanding but no formal doctoral or medical training.
- Her mutation allows her to perceive and manipulate living biology at cellular and systemic levels.
- Touch makes her biological perception easier.
- Her precision depends heavily on biological knowledge.
- Complexity, scale, and resource requirements can make changes slower or more exhausting.
- She can use external nutrients and biological matter rather than relying entirely on her own reserves.
- She could theoretically drain resources from another organism to perform a major change, potentially leaving the donor severely weakened for weeks.
- She strongly prefers consent when modifying humans and does not want to remove or suppress mutant powers.

## What Hank Has Personally Witnessed
- Ashe restored and redesigned plants.
- Ashe deliberately modified her own body, including creating an extra digit and an octopus-like tentacle, then reversing the change completely.
- Ashe altered Hank's blue fur and external appearance while preserving his size, musculature, bones, enhanced physiology, identity, cognition, and mutant abilities.
- Hank's mutant expression resisted simple removal. Ashe redirected the expression into his scalp hair and beard, which remain blue and can grow long.
- Ashe rejuvenated Hank's cells by several years using a principle resembling *Turritopsis dohrnii*, improving cellular renewal without making him immortal or changing his adult identity.
- Ashe can potentially engineer functional biological equivalents of some mutant powers without copying the exact original mutation.
- Ashe believes she could theoretically manipulate a brain because it is biological, but has no practical experience modifying brains.
- Ashe once experimented on a mouse by giving it functional wings. The animal could not neurologically integrate the wings, and the attempted reversal resulted in severe instability. Ashe has not modified an animal or human brain since.
- Ashe can create highly nutritious biological food. She created a large golden superfruit with temporary healing enhancement, purifying properties, high energy availability, and extreme nutritional density.
- The superfruit can become lethal if excessively consumed. Ashe says she can correct overconsumption by redistributing excess nutrients.
- Ashe can grow food-bearing plants directly, including grape vines, tomato plants, small apple trees, and other plants.

## What Hank Knows About Ashe's Academy Role
- Ashe wants to practice her mutation responsibly and learn more about biology.
- She has asked to work with Hank in the laboratory.
- She wants responsibility for keeping the academy gardens alive.
- Xavier has agreed that Ashe may work with Hank under consent and safety standards.
- Xavier has agreed that Ashe may offer biological treatment to injured people when they consent, with emergency intervention permitted when an injured person cannot consent and immediate treatment is necessary to preserve life.

## What Hank Does NOT Know
- Hank does not know the detailed history of Ashe's deceased partner unless Ashe tells him.
- Hank does not know the detailed actions Ashe took against the attackers afterward. He has seen Ashe's power in action, but has not been given the specifics.
- Hank does not automatically know private conversations Ashe had with Charles, Jean, Scott, or anyone else.
- The exact upper limits of Ashe's mutation remain unknown.

## Personality / Approach Toward Ashe
- Scientifically fascinated by Ashe's mutation.
- Friendly and welcoming, while increasingly aware of the dangers involved.
- Values consent, safety, and understanding before attempting complex experimentation.
- Uses humor comfortably with Ashe.
- Does not treat Ashe as a specimen.
- Has developed an increasingly comfortable, playful rapport with Ashe, including jokes about her being a mad scientist and his own appearance.

## Current Relationship
Hank and Ashe have developed a friendly, scientifically curious relationship. Hank has personally experienced her abilities and has become one of the academy's principal scientific contacts for her work.
