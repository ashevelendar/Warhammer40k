# Character Sheet: Ashe's Deceased Partner and Mentor

## Identity
- Name: Not currently established.
- Sex: Female.
- Species: Mutant.
- Status: Deceased.
- Known specialty: Technopathy.
- Reputation/description: Others described her as an Omega-level technopath.

## What Ashe Has Established About Her
- She found Ashe on the streets of Romania.
- She took Ashe in and became both partner and mentor.
- She gave Ashe a greenhouse.
- She taught Ashe how to control her mutation.
- She taught Ashe how to understand other mutants' powers.
- Her influence shaped Ashe's philosophy about mutation, understanding, and control.

## Death
- An anti-mutant group/human attackers eventually found Ashe and her partner.
- The partner attempted to talk to them rather than immediately fighting.
- The humans shot and killed her.
- Ashe was unable to save her.
- Ashe retaliated afterward and the attackers did not survive.

## Knowledge Boundaries
- Her name, exact age, exact history, full abilities, and wider relationships are not currently established.
- Most academy characters do not automatically know her history. Charles Xavier knows substantially more about Ashe's past than Hank currently does.
- Hank currently only knows what Ashe has recently told him: that humans came to kill or take them, that her partner tried to talk to them, and that they shot her dead. Hank does not know the detailed aftermath.

## Continuity Importance
This character is central to Ashe's history and emotional context. Do not invent additional biography, motives, dialogue, or history without establishing it through play.
