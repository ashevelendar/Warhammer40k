# Character Sheet: Charles Xavier

## Role
- Founder and head of Xavier's Academy.
- Senior telepath and mentor figure within the academy.

## What Charles Knows About Ashe
- He personally contacted Ashe and invited her to the academy.
- He knows Ashe's traumatic history involving her deceased female mutant partner/mentor and the anti-mutant humans who attacked them.
- He knows the partner was an exceptionally skilled technopath who took Ashe in, gave her a greenhouse, and taught her how to understand and control her mutation.
- He knows the partner was shot and killed by the attackers while attempting to talk to them.
- He knows Ashe retaliated afterward and that the attackers did not survive.
- He knows Ashe carries guilt surrounding the incident and fears losing control.
- He knows Ashe does not want to be treated as a weapon and has expressed a boundary against being ordered to kill.
- He knows Ashe does not want to modify humans without consent.
- He has seen enough of Ashe's abilities to understand that her biological manipulation is extraordinarily powerful.

## What Charles Has Seen / Established
- Ashe can manipulate living biology at cellular and systemic levels.
- Her precision depends strongly on knowledge and understanding.
- Complex biological work can exhaust her severely.
- Ashe prefers helping and healing over using her mutation as a weapon.
- Ashe is interested in learning and has identified knowledge as a major limiter on her abilities.

## What Charles Does NOT Automatically Know
- Anything Ashe has learned, discovered, experimented with, or discussed privately after arriving unless he witnessed it, was told about it, or otherwise learned it in-world.
- Ashe's private thoughts or intentions.
- Details of any new application of her mutation that he has not observed or been told about.
- The full limits of her mutation.

## Relationship With Ashe
Charles has offered Ashe a place where she can decide what to do with her abilities rather than being defined as a threat or weapon. He has not promised to absolve her of her past, but he has listened to her account without simply condemning her.

## Continuity Note
Charles has more knowledge of Ashe's past than Hank currently does. Do not transfer Charles's knowledge to Hank, Jean, Scott, or other characters without an in-world reason.
