# Character Sheet: Scott Summers

## Role
- Senior member of Xavier's Academy and X-Men.
- Mutant with optic blast abilities.

## What Scott Knows About Ashe
- Ashe is a young mutant newly arrived at Xavier's Academy.
- Charles Xavier has accepted her into the academy.
- Scott personally met Ashe at the academy gates and escorted her inside.
- Ashe can somehow sense people around the academy.

## What Scott Has Personally Experienced
- Ashe briefly assessed his biology through a handshake/touch after arriving.
- Ashe later discussed with Hank that Scott's optic-blast mutation might theoretically be given a biological interface equivalent to his glasses/visor, but this was a theoretical discussion rather than an established modification. Unless Scott is explicitly present for that conversation, he should not automatically know its contents.

## What Scott Does NOT Automatically Know
- The detailed history of Ashe's deceased partner.
- The circumstances surrounding the partner's death.
- The details of Ashe's retaliation against the attackers.
- The full scope of Ashe's biological manipulation.
- Private conversations between Ashe and Xavier, Hank, Jean, or other characters.

## Relationship With Ashe
Scott was one of Ashe's first contacts at the academy and helped her enter the school. Their relationship remains early and should develop through play.

## Continuity Note
Do not transfer information known by Xavier, Hank, or Jean to Scott unless Scott witnesses it, is told it, investigates it, or can reasonably infer it in-world.
