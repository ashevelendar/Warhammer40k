# Ur'gal, Current Canonical State

## Identity
- Name: Ur'gal
- Species: Kobold
- Stage: Tribe
- Population: 48
- Government: Chieftainship
- Leader: Ashe, 18, female, Chieftan
- Immediate ambition: Found the tribe properly

## Origin
The families of Ur'gal were previously separate. They found one another, chose to remain together, and became a unified tribe. Ashe is a relatively new addition who has already demonstrated leadership by guiding them to a place where they could settle, even if temporarily.

## Culture
- It takes a tribe to raise a child.
- Children are raised collectively by the whole tribe.
- Mother and father do not exist as social roles.
- Biological parentage is socially irrelevant.
- Kobolds are small and physically weak, but fast and reproduce faster than average.
- Clothing is improvised from whatever is available, or sometimes absent.
- The tribe does not yet know how to properly skin animals.

## Faith
### The Sightless King
God of the Blind. Worshippers bind their eyes to lose their sight, believing blindness allows them to see better in another sense.

### The Flint-Sparks, The Twin Breaths
Two fleeting deities that exist in the split-second when two stones strike together.
- Spark that catches tinder: God of Warmth.
- Spark that dies in the air: God of Cold.

## Current Circumstances
Ur'gal has only just become a unified tribe and has reached a location where it can settle, at least briefly. The surrounding world is largely unknown. Infrastructure and technology are primitive.

## Development
Current civilization stage: Tribe.
Next intended stage: City, requiring appropriate technological, infrastructural, food-security, population, and organizational development.

## Technology
Ur'gal begins with rudimentary practical knowledge. Technology must be learned, discovered, preserved, taught, or acquired. Advancement is cumulative and prerequisite-based.
