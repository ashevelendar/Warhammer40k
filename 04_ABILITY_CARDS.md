# Ability Card: Ashe Ben'Ghazad - Overhaul-Type Mutation

## Core Mutation
Ashe possesses a mutant ability analogous in broad function to Overhaul: through physical contact, he can disassemble and reconstruct matter.

## Demonstrated Capabilities
- Disassemble and reconstruct matter through touch.
- Manipulate living biological tissue.
- Alter internal anatomy with lethal precision.
- Reconstruct bodies at cellular level.
- Reconstruct a corpse into a body physically indistinguishable from Ashe at the cellular level.
- Modify his own physical structure.

## Development Philosophy
The mutation should be treated as powerful but not omnipotent. Its exact limits, costs, range, precision, speed, required understanding, vulnerabilities, and possible advanced applications are discovered through play.

Do not silently grant Ashe an application merely because the power could theoretically support it. A new technique becomes established only after Ashe demonstrates, learns, or explicitly develops it in-world.

## Known Training Influence
Ashe's deceased former partner, an older mutant technopath, taught him how to use his mutation and how to think about it. Her influence is part of Ashe's established history, but her full identity, methods, and knowledge are not yet established.

## Knowledge Boundary
Characters in the world do not automatically know the full extent of Ashe's mutation. What they know depends on what they have witnessed, been told, investigated, or can reasonably infer.
