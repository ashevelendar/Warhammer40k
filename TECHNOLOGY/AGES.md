# Ages of Civilization

A realm advances through societal stages by developing the knowledge, institutions, infrastructure, population, and capabilities required for the next stage.

Initial stages:

1. Tribe
2. City
3. Nation

Further stages may emerge naturally as the world develops. They are not guaranteed to exist in this exact form.

## Advancement principles

Advancement is not simply a population threshold.

A realm may need combinations of:

- Specific technologies
- Sufficient infrastructure
- Administrative capability
- Food security
- Economic capacity
- Population concentration
- Social cohesion
- Institutions
- Political legitimacy
- Appropriate historical circumstances

Advancing to a new stage changes what the realm can realistically build, administer, research, and sustain.

A realm can also stagnate, fracture, regress, or lose capabilities. Advancement never guarantees stability.
