# Technology & Knowledge

Technology is a cumulative part of realm development. A realm does not automatically know a technology because another civilization has it.

## Principles

- Knowledge must be discovered, learned, preserved, taught, or acquired.
- Technologies have prerequisites and may unlock further discoveries.
- Research capacity is influenced by population, institutions, specialists, infrastructure, culture, and circumstances.
- Some knowledge can be lost through catastrophe, isolation, death of specialists, or destruction of institutions.
- Magic is uncommon. Magical discoveries therefore form a separate branch of knowledge rather than replacing ordinary technological development.
- Technologies can have social, economic, military, infrastructural, cultural, and diplomatic consequences.

## Broad branches

- Agriculture & Food
- Materials & Craft
- Construction & Infrastructure
- Warfare
- Governance & Administration
- Trade & Economics
- Navigation & Exploration
- Medicine & Biology
- Scholarship & Education
- Magic & Arcane Knowledge
- Culture & Communication

The detailed tree is maintained in the database so that prerequisites, costs, effects, discoveries, and realm-specific progress can evolve without requiring the human-readable record to contain every numerical value.
