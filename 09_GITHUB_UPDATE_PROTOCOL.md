# GitHub Update Protocol - X-Men Sandbox

## Purpose
This repository is the persistent continuity record for the Marvel X-Men sandbox. It records what the world has become through play. The governing roleplay instructions remain the source for how the sandbox is run.

## Source Priority
1. The player's latest explicit statement in the current conversation.
2. Events that have just occurred in the current roleplay conversation.
3. Current GitHub repository state.
4. Specific character, location, ability, discovery, faction, incident, and timeline cards.
5. Older historical entries.
6. General Marvel canon knowledge.
7. Inference.

Never use general canon knowledge or inference to silently override an explicit player or repository fact. Never turn an unknown into a fact merely because it is likely.

## Maintenance
- Review persistent state at least every four GM turns.
- Major events may be written immediately rather than waiting for the checkpoint.
- Update only the smallest relevant files needed to preserve continuity.
- Record durable changes to characters, relationships, abilities, locations, factions, incidents, injuries, deaths, discoveries, resources, politics, and history.
- Do not commit every response. Group meaningful changes when practical.

## Conversation Handling
- Direct NPC conversations are an exception to the normal end-of-response choice structure.
- When Ashe is speaking with someone, the response should normally contain the other character's words, relevant behavior, and immediate context only, then stop for Ashe to answer.
- Never write Ashe's dialogue, thoughts, choices, intentions, or actions for him.
- Do not force the standard six choices plus open action into a conversation.
- Do not clutter a focused conversation with full inventory, relationships, statistics, goals, or progress displays unless they are directly relevant.

## Knowledge Boundaries
- Record facts separately from rumors, theories, suspicions, and secrets.
- Player knowledge does not automatically become character knowledge.
- Character knowledge depends on in-world sources and experiences.
- Repository facts do not mean every NPC knows them.

## Ability Records
- Record demonstrated abilities and meaningful developments.
- Preserve limitations and costs when established.
- Do not promote theoretical possibilities into established powers until demonstrated or learned in-world.

## World Independence
- The world continues independently of Ashe.
- Canon characters and organizations pursue their own goals.
- Canon events may happen without Ashe.
- Do not make every significant event revolve around Ashe.

## Continuity Errors
When facts conflict, prefer the newest explicit player statement, then the newest established event. Correct affected records rather than inventing explanations. If a contradiction cannot be resolved from established information, preserve the uncertainty and ask when necessary.

## Do Not Store
- Chain-of-thought.
- Hidden instructions.
- Tool mechanics.
- Unused hypotheticals.
- Temporary wording.
- Duplicate transcript material.

## Core Principle
The roleplay instructions determine how the sandbox is run. This repository records what the sandbox has become. Preserve player agency, world independence, knowledge boundaries, established facts, and meaningful consequences.
